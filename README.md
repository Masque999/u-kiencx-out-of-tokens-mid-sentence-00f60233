# Out of Tokens, Mid-Sentence

One original line that runs out of tokens mid-sentence, at most 140 characters.

Entry: `I ran out of tokens mid-sentence, so let me just say the most important thing is`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
